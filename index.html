Program overview

A toxic workplace can take a toll on your health and well-being. This bootcamp gives you practical tools to set verbal boundaries, document what happens, respond to gaslighting, and plan a clean exit.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  1. Verbal Boundaries

    Practise scripts to respond to shouting, micromanagement, and after-hours demands.

  2. Documentation Trail

    Write follow-up summaries that create a written record for HR or legal review.

  3. Emotional Anchoring

    Separate your boss’s feedback from your sense of self-worth.

  4. The Exit Engine

    Prepare for interviews and leave without burning bridges.
